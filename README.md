# Nithin Namani — UX Designer

I design and test product experiences end to end — from the first user conversation to the feature that actually ships. My focus: UX research, design systems, accessibility (WCAG 2.1), and e-commerce & fintech products.

## What I do

- Ran 50+ user interviews and usability sessions, and I design from what people actually say — not what I assumed they'd say.
- Shipped 15+ features across iOS, Android, and web.
- Built a 100+ component Figma design system that 4 product teams adopted and still use.
- Run A/B tests that lifted CTR by 15%.
- Run accessibility audits that brought products to 95% WCAG 2.1 compliance.

## The UX Lab

My portfolio, [nithinnamani.com](https://nithinnamani.com), is where the work lives. I call it the UX Lab because every project gets written up the way it actually happened — the first guess, what the research turned up, the versions that failed testing, and what made the final cut.

- **Lab Reports 01–06** — six case studies covering the full evidence-to-outcome arc: interviews, surveys, affinity mapping, competitive analysis, fidelity rounds, usability testing, results.
- **Product Design series** — five commerce concepts, each with its own motion-design system: Nike, PlayStation 5, Skylark (drone commerce), Lumen (camera commerce), and Volt (e-bike commerce).

## What keeps me going

I talk to users before I sketch. I test my guesses against evidence instead of arguing about opinions. Motion design is my favorite craft detail — when it stays inside its own screen and explains the product, it earns its place. And I train six days a week; the discipline carries straight into the work.
